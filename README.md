### Project Overview
I transformed 50,000 raw car sales transactions into an interactive Excel dashboard to uncover sales trends, evaluate dealership performance, and generate actionable business recommendations.

The dataset covers five dealership branches over 18 months, from January 2022 to June 2023. It includes vehicle brands, models, selling prices, discounts, financing methods, insurance, and customer demographics.

This project demonstrates how Excel can be used to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights that support data-driven decision-making.

### Project Objectives
- Analyze overall revenue and average vehicle selling prices.
- Identify the highest-revenue vehicle brands.
- Compare dealership branch performance.
- Evaluate the relationship between discounts and final selling prices.
- Analyze customer purchasing patterns by gender.
- Compare vehicle purchases by payment and financing method.
- Develop an interactive dashboard for business reporting.

### Tools
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning and Preparation
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Excel Formulas
- Interactive Slicers
- Dashboard Design

 ### Project Workflow

1. Data Cleaning: Removed blank and broken records and structured the transactional dataset for analysis.
2. Data Organization: Prepared the data for consistent calculations and reporting.
3. Exploratory Analysis: Used PivotTables to investigate revenue, brands, branches, discounts, financing, and customer demographics.
4. Dashboard Development: Designed a one-page interactive dashboard featuring nine linked charts and slicers.
5. Data Validation: Used Excel formulas to cross-check summary figures against the source data.
6. Business Recommendations: Translated the findings into practical recommendations for dealership management.

### Key Findings
- Total tracked revenue: LKR 422.45 billion.
- Average sale value: Approximately LKR 8.45 million per vehicle.
- Top-performing branch: Negombo Auto World recorded the highest average sale value.
- Leading brand: Nissan generated the highest revenue among the six brands tracked, followed by Hyundai and Honda.
- Financing insights: Customers using bank loans had a slightly higher average purchase value than cash and leasing customers.
- Customer demographics: Average spending was nearly identical between male and female customers.
- Discount analysis: The discount rate showed almost no consistent relationship with the final sale price.
- Yearly comparison: The available data covers only six months of 2023, so a full-year comparison would be needed before drawing firm conclusions about annual performance.

### Business Recommendations
1. Based on the analysis, I recommend the following actions:
2. Improve branch performance: Investigate the practices behind Negombo Auto World's higher average sale value and assess whether they can be replicated at other branches.
3. Prioritize leading brands: Review inventory allocation and marketing strategies for Nissan, Hyundai, and Honda based on their revenue contribution.
4. Make discounts strategic: Link discounts to specific objectives, such as clearing older stock, instead of applying them without a clear purpose.
5. Strengthen banking partnerships: Investigate opportunities to improve financing options for customers who prefer bank loans.
6. Avoid unnecessary gender-based targeting: Use more meaningful customer behavior and purchasing indicators rather than gender alone.
7. Improve time-period comparisons: Compare equivalent periods before drawing conclusions about annual sales growth or decline.
8. Analyze profitability: Extend the analysis to include acquisition costs, operating expenses, and profit margins before making final inventory decisions.

### Key Takeaway
This project strengthened my ability to manage the complete data analytics workflow:

Clean → Structure → Analyze → Visualize → Communicate

It also reinforced an important lesson: data analysis is not just about producing charts. It is about asking the right business questions, validating the numbers, identifying meaningful patterns, and communicating findings that can support better decisions.
